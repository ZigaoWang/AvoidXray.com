# Part Two: Privacy Policy

## 1. Where your data is

- **The site, its database and its analytics** run on one server in Los Angeles, United States.
- **Image files and database backups** are stored on Aliyun OSS in Hong Kong.
- **The site is run** by Zigao Wang, in Shanghai.

If you are in mainland China, your information leaves it as soon as you use the site. Ticking the box at sign-up is your consent to that.

## 2. What we don't do

- **We don't sell your information** or share it for anyone's marketing.
- **We don't run ads**, so nothing here builds a profile of you for advertisers.
- **We don't use third-party trackers.** No ad cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics company.
- **We don't claim your work.** See [Part One](#2-your-photos-stay-yours).
- **Your original files are yours alone.** We keep each file exactly as you upload it, and only you can view or download it. Everything other people see is a separate copy with no metadata, so posting a frame doesn't post where you stood or who you are.

## 3. What we collect

**Your account.** Email address, username, display name and password. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, which we can't read.

**Your profile, if you fill it in.** Bio, avatar, website and social handles. All optional, all public.

**Your photos.** The image files, plus the camera, film stock, caption and date you tag them with.

**What your photos show.** Software on our own server looks at each photo you upload. It records a description of the image as numbers, used so search can find photos by what is in them and to suggest similar photos, and a score for whether it contains nudity. These stay on our server and are deleted with the photo. No photo is sent to an outside service for this.

**What you write.** Comments, community notes, album names and descriptions, and anything you send through the feedback or report forms. If you send feedback while signed out, we also keep the email address you give so we can reply.

**What you do on the site.** Follows, likes, blocks, and the notifications those create.

**Usage.** We run our own copy of Umami, an open source analytics tool, on our own server. It records which pages are viewed, the referring site, browser, operating system, device type and country. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites. Admin pages are never recorded.

**Technical.** Your IP address, used to run the site, find faults and limit abuse.

### Cookies and browser storage

We set two cookies: one that keeps you signed in, and one that remembers you dismissed the getting-started prompt on your profile. Your browser also keeps some things in its own storage, like where you were in a photo grid, so going back returns you to the same place. None of it is used to track you.

### Photo metadata

Image files often carry EXIF metadata: camera or scanner model, exposure settings, timestamps, and sometimes GPS coordinates. Lab scans usually carry the scanner's details and no location. Phone photos often carry GPS.

**We keep your original file exactly as you uploaded it, metadata included, and only you can view or download it.** That includes location data, if your camera or scanner recorded any. Nobody else is given the original, whether or not they can see the photo. What other people see, and what they can download or export, are separate copies we make from it for display, up to 4000 pixels on the long side. Those copies carry no metadata at all: no GPS coordinates, serial numbers, names, camera or scanner details, timestamps or editing history.

We read the scanner or camera model from your file and keep it with the photo, where only you can see it. We never take a photo's date from the file, because a scan's date is when it was scanned, not when it was shot.

If you'd rather we didn't keep the metadata at all, remove it before uploading.

**Never public:** your email address, your password, photos and albums you've set to private, who you've blocked, and reports you've filed. Private photos can be opened by you and by the site's administrator, who may need to for moderation.

**The nudity check.** Each upload is checked before it is stored. A photo the check flags is not stored at all, unless you ask for a review; it is then stored, and only you and the administrator can see it until the administrator decides. If the check cannot run, the photo is stored and checked shortly after, and stays hidden from others until then.

Search engines index public pages. When someone shares a link, the other platform shows and caches a preview image.
