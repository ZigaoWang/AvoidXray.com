# Part Two: Privacy Policy

## 1. Where your photographs live

- **The site and its database** run on a server in Los Angeles, United States.
- **Your image files** are stored on Aliyun OSS in Hong Kong.
- **AvoidXray is run by one person**, Zigao Wang, based in Shanghai.

If you are in mainland China, your information leaves it as soon as you use the site. Ticking the box at sign-up is your consent to that.

## 2. What we don't do

- **We don't sell your personal information**, and we don't share it for anyone else's marketing.
- **No advertising**, so nothing here is built to profile you for advertisers.
- **No third-party analytics.** No tracking pixels, no ad cookies. The only cookies we set keep you signed in and stop abuse.
- **We don't claim your work.** You keep copyright in your photographs — see [Part One](#2-your-photos-stay-yours).
- **We strip GPS coordinates** out of image files before storing them, so publishing a frame doesn't publish where you stood.

## 3. What we collect

**Account.** Email address, username, display name, password. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and we can't read them.

**Profile, if you add it.** Bio, avatar, website and social links. All optional and public.

**Photos and what you tag them with.** The image files, plus camera, film stock, caption, and date taken.

**What you write.** Comments, Community Notes, album descriptions, and anything sent through the feedback or report forms.

**Technical.** IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit — used to run the site, diagnose faults, and rate-limit abuse.

**Cookies.** Only what's needed to keep you signed in and to protect against abuse.

### Photo metadata

Image files often carry EXIF metadata: camera or scanner model, exposure settings, timestamps, and sometimes GPS coordinates recording where the frame was taken. Lab scans usually carry the scanner's details and no location. Photographs taken on a phone very often carry GPS.

**We remove GPS coordinates before storing an image.** A file carrying them is re-encoded, which removes its other metadata too. A file without them — which is nearly every lab scan — is stored exactly as you uploaded it, scanner details and all. Anyone can download that file from the photo page. We don't display any of it.

If you'd rather we held none of it, strip metadata before uploading.

Public to anyone, signed in or not: your username, display name, avatar, bio and links; photos you've published with the camera and film stock you tagged; your comments and Notes; your follower and following counts.

Never public: your email address, your password, photos you've marked private, who you've blocked, and reports you've filed.

Search engines index public pages. Shared links produce a preview image on the other platform, which caches it beyond our control.

Each processes data for us and not for their own purposes. Otherwise we disclose personal information only where legally required, or where it's needed to protect someone's safety or investigate abuse of the site.

If AvoidXray ever changes hands we'll tell you before your information moves.

- **Account, photos, and posts:** until you delete them, or ask us to delete your account.
- **Deleted content:** removed from the site immediately. Copies others have already downloaded or that other platforms have cached can't be recalled.
- **Reports and moderation records:** kept after the content is gone, so repeat problems can be recognised.

Email a@zigao.wang and we'll respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm you control the account first, because we can't let someone else delete your photographs.

## 9. Security

Hashed passwords, HTTPS, and access controls limiting who can reach the production database. No service is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information we'll notify you and the relevant authorities as required.

## 10. Children

You must be at least 14 to create an account, and under 18s need a parent or guardian's permission.

We're based in Shanghai, so China's Personal Information Protection Law applies. It treats the personal information of children under 14 as sensitive and requires a guardian's consent, which we aren't set up to collect — so 14 is a real limit rather than a formality. Sign-up asks you to confirm you meet it; we have no way to verify it beyond that. If you believe a younger child has an account, contact a@zigao.wang and we'll remove it.
